A Private Retreat in the Heart of Westport

Published November 12, 2026 · Westport Family Homes

With 2.27 acres, a heated saltwater pool, expansive outdoor living spaces, and room for a tennis court, 5 Baldwin Place offers a different kind of everyday luxury.

At the end of a quiet cul-de-sac in Westport, a tree-lined approach leads to a newly completed home that makes the most of its surroundings.

The property at 5 Baldwin Place occupies 2.27 acres, with broad lawns, mature trees, and outdoor amenities that give the residence the atmosphere of a private retreat.

Built by Westport Family Homes, the six-bedroom, eight-bathroom residence offers 9,062 square feet of finished living space. But much of what distinguishes the property lies beyond its walls.

An Outdoor Setting Made for Living

The centerpiece of the backyard is a 20-by-40-foot heated saltwater pool with an integrated spa.

A covered terrace and fully equipped outdoor kitchen create a comfortable setting for meals and gatherings, while open-air patios provide additional room to enjoy the grounds.

The property’s generous acreage also leaves space for future additions. Buyers interested in tennis or pickleball have room to explore a private court, while the possibility of an accessory dwelling unit offers another dimension of flexibility, subject to applicable approvals.

Together, these features create opportunities for a lifestyle that extends well beyond the traditional backyard.

Where Indoor and Outdoor Living Meet

Inside, the home’s design is equally suited to entertaining.

The kitchen centers around a 12-foot stone counter with custom decorative metal inlay. A separate scullery and walk-in pantry provide functional preparation and storage space, allowing the main kitchen to remain a gathering place.

Nearby, a glass-walled, climate-controlled wine room accommodates approximately 300 bottles.

The great room opens toward the outdoor living areas, connecting the interior with the terrace and grounds. Formal living and dining rooms offer additional settings for gatherings, from quiet dinners to larger celebrations.

The walkout lower level adds another dimension to the experience, with a family room and bar, media space, gym, and direct access toward the pool.

Throughout the home, thoughtful details bring warmth to its generous scale. The primary bedroom features a vaulted ceiling with custom oak beams and a linear fireplace, while its bathroom includes heated floors and spa-inspired finishes.

A Private Setting With Westport Close By

Despite the property’s secluded atmosphere, 5 Baldwin Place remains connected to the wider Westport community.

Downtown shopping, restaurants, Compo Beach, and Metro-North rail service are all part of the lifestyle available in this coastal Connecticut town.

For buyers seeking new construction, substantial acreage, and a home where entertaining and everyday living come together naturally, 5 Baldwin Place offers a compelling combination.

And unlike a home still under construction, the residence is complete and ready for immediate occupancy.
