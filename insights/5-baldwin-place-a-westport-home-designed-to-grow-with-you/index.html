5 Baldwin Place

A Westport Home Designed to Grow With You

Published November 12, 2026 · Westport Family Homes

At 5 Baldwin Place, four finished levels offer room for everyday family life, visiting relatives, entertaining, and whatever the years ahead may bring.

A home that works beautifully today may not be the home a family needs ten or twenty years from now. Children grow older, parents come to visit for longer stays, work moves home, and the way people use their living spaces changes.

At 5 Baldwin Place in Westport, Connecticut, that evolution was considered from the beginning.

Built by award-winning Westport Family Homes, the newly completed residence offers six bedrooms, eight bathrooms, and 9,062 square feet across four finished levels. Set on 2.27 acres at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac, it combines generous shared spaces with areas designed for greater independence and privacy.

Space to Be Together, and Space to Be Apart

The main level brings everyone together. A spacious kitchen with a 12-foot stone counter opens to a great room, creating a natural gathering place for meals, conversation, and daily life. Formal living and dining rooms provide additional space for entertaining, while a private office with windows on three sides offers a quiet place to work.

Upstairs, the primary suite features a vaulted oak-beamed ceiling, a fireplace, and a spa-inspired bathroom with heated floors. Four additional bedrooms occupy the same level, keeping the home’s principal sleeping quarters together.

But some of the most interesting spaces are on the floors above and below.

The finished third level includes a playroom and bathroom, providing a separate area that can evolve as younger children become teenagers.

On the walkout lower level, a sixth bedroom and full bathroom sit alongside a family room, media and play space, and gym. The arrangement offers visiting relatives or extended family greater privacy while keeping everyone under one roof.

Designed With the Future in Mind

The home’s flexibility extends beyond its current floor plan.

An elevator-ready design anticipates changing mobility needs. Outside, the expansive grounds offer room for a future accessory dwelling unit, potentially creating another way to accommodate guests or extended family, subject to applicable approvals.

There is also room for a tennis court, while the existing heated saltwater pool, spa, and outdoor kitchen make the property equally appealing for everyday enjoyment.

At its heart, 5 Baldwin Place reflects a simple idea: a well-designed home should adapt to the people living in it, rather than require them to adapt to the house.

With new construction already complete and immediate occupancy available, the next chapter can begin without waiting for a home to be built.
