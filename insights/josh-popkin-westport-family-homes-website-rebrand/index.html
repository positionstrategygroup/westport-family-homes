How Josh Popkin Helped Rebrand Westport Family Homes

Published July 20, 2026 · Westport Family Homes

Project strategy and website rebrand by Josh Popkin, founder of Position Strategy Group.

Westport Family Homes partnered with Josh Popkin, founder of Position Strategy Group, to replace its legacy website with a new digital experience built around the company’s homes, history, and connection to Westport, Connecticut.

Josh led the website strategy, brand positioning, messaging, visual direction, content structure, and overall digital experience. The project also had a personal connection: Josh is the son of David Popkin, founder and owner of Westport Family Homes.

The result is a new website and visual identity that gives Westport Family Homes a clearer way to present its approach to luxury homebuilding, family-centered design, craftsmanship, and the community where it builds.

Project Overview

  • Client: Westport Family Homes
  • Project: Website rebrand and digital experience redesign
  • Lead strategist: Josh Popkin, Position Strategy Group
  • Location: Westport, Connecticut
  • Scope: Website strategy, brand positioning, messaging, visual direction, content structure, and digital experience
  • Featured property: 5 Baldwin Place

Replacing a Legacy Website

Before the redesign, Westport Family Homes had a website that no longer reflected the quality, craftsmanship, and reputation behind the company’s work.

The company needed a website that could help prospective buyers understand more than the homes themselves. It needed to communicate:

  • Who Westport Family Homes is
  • How the company approaches homebuilding
  • What makes its homes different
  • Where the company builds
  • Which properties are available
  • How prospective buyers can begin a conversation

The redesign created a clearer structure for the company’s history, projects, building philosophy, current opportunities, and connection to Westport families.

Josh Popkin’s Role in the Rebrand

Josh Popkin and Position Strategy Group led the strategic and creative work behind the rebrand.

His responsibilities included:

  • Website strategy
  • Brand positioning
  • Messaging
  • Content structure
  • Visual direction
  • Digital experience design
  • Website architecture
  • Alignment between the company’s logo, properties, and local identity

Josh’s work focused on connecting the company’s business goals with the way visitors experience the website. The new structure gives prospective buyers clearer paths to learn about Westport Family Homes, explore its properties, understand its building approach, and contact the team.

The project also required the new website to communicate the company’s local identity. Westport Family Homes is closely connected to Westport, Connecticut, so the brand needed to feel specific to the community rather than interchangeable with any luxury homebuilder.

A Website Strategy Built Around Clarity

The new website is organized around the information prospective buyers need most:

  • The company’s history
  • Its approach to building homes
  • Completed projects
  • Current properties
  • Features and amenities
  • The local communities it serves
  • A direct path to contact the team

This structure helps the website work as both a brand experience and a practical resource for people researching luxury homebuilders in Westport and Fairfield County.

The website also gives search engines clearer context about the company, its location, its services, its properties, and the subjects it covers.

A New Visual Identity for Westport Family Homes

The rebrand introduced a new logo inspired by Westport’s coastal setting and sense of place.

The lighthouse logo symbolizes guidance, connection, and direction. Those ideas reflect both the character of Westport and the experience Westport Family Homes wants clients to have during the homebuilding process.

The previous identity focused primarily on the company’s initials. The new identity connects the company more directly to Westport and the setting where its homes are built.

A Website Built Around the Homes

The redesigned website presents Westport Family Homes through its history, building philosophy, completed projects, and current opportunities.

The website prominently features 5 Baldwin Place, a new-construction luxury home in Westport. Visitors can explore the property’s design, setting, amenities, and family-oriented living spaces.

The site also presents 5 Baldwin Place alongside completed Westport Family Homes properties. This gives prospective buyers a broader view of the company’s work, design approach, and craftsmanship.

The new structure helps visitors move from learning about the company to exploring its homes and contacting the team.

Creating a More Personal Digital Experience

Luxury homebuilding is a personal process. Buyers evaluate the home, but they also evaluate the builder, the design approach, the location, the communication process, and the experience of working together.

The new website is designed to make those parts of the experience easier to understand.

Its photography, project presentation, messaging, and navigation create a more direct connection between the visitor and the company. The website gives prospective buyers a better sense of the homes Westport Family Homes builds and the people behind the work.

The Connection Between Branding and Search Visibility

Josh’s work on the project also connected the brand strategy to the website’s content and information structure.

A strong website needs to communicate clearly to both people and search engines. That means making it easy to understand:

  • What the company does
  • Where it works
  • Who it serves
  • Which services it provides
  • Which properties it has built
  • How its work differs from other builders

The rebrand brought those ideas together through the website’s page structure, messaging, internal links, property content, and visual presentation.

This gives Westport Family Homes a stronger foundation for searches related to luxury homes, custom homebuilding, Westport, Connecticut, Fairfield County, and family-centered home design.

A Family Connection Behind the Project

The rebrand was also a collaboration between father and son.

David Popkin founded Westport Family Homes and continues to lead the company as its owner and builder. Josh Popkin brought his professional experience in marketing, SEO, branding, website strategy, and digital experiences to the project.

Because Josh grew up around the business, he understood the company’s history and the standards behind its work. That background helped connect the new direction to the company’s roots while giving the brand a more current presentation.

David Popkin said:

“What I enjoyed most about working with Josh Popkin and Position Strategy Group was how easy they made the entire process. They helped transform my outdated legacy website into a modern, polished site that truly reflects the quality of the business I have built.” David Popkin, Owner & Builder, Westport Family Homes

About Westport Family Homes
