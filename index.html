Built by a Neighbor

David PopkinOwner & Builder
Lori PopkinReal Estate Broker

Owner David Popkin has lived in Westport for more than 30 years with his wife and three children, all of whom came up through the Westport school system. He has been building homes here for almost two decades — this is his town, and every home is built with that in mind.

As a LEED Certified professional, David stays current with the latest building and environmental techniques — so every home meets the quality, design, and energy-efficiency standards today's homeowners expect.

Westport Family Homes has been honored with four HOBI Awards from the Home Building Industry Association of Connecticut — recognition from the industry for the homes we're proudest of.

David holds an undergraduate degree from Brandeis University and an MBA from Boston University, and currently serves as Vice Chairman of the Board of the Connecticut/Westchester Crohn's and Colitis Foundation.

"Every home we build is designed the way a family actually lives — with the quality, comfort, and efficiency to last for decades."

— David Popkin, Owner
